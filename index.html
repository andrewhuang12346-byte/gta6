The Plot

Welcome to Leonida

Two outlaws, one easy score, and the least reliable getaway driver in the state.

Lucia is fresh out of Leonida Penitentiary. Jason wants out of the game. Together they're planning one “easy” score.

Every heist needs a getaway driver. The only one who accepts the ride is Elon: a 3am rideshare driver who swears he “used to run a few companies” and quotes every ETA as “two weeks.”

His ride is a stainless-steel wedge with one shattered “unbreakable” window, panel gaps you could post a letter through, and a self-driving mode he can't switch off. The cops are chasing a supercar, a speedboat… and a pickup that keeps rerouting to the nearest charging station.

Nobody planned for him. He planned for Mars.
